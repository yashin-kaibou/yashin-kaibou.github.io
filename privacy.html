日本語

yashin-uploader は、YouTube チャンネル「野心の解剖室」（@yashin_kaibou）の持ち主が、自分の YouTube チャンネルに動画・サムネイル・字幕を上げるためだけに使う道具です。

  • Google の許可の鍵（OAuth トークン）は、持ち主の手元のコンピュータにだけ保存し、外部のサーバーには送りません。

  • 許可はいつでも Google アカウントの設定 から取り消せます。

English

yashin-uploader is a tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Yashin Kaibou" (@yashin_kaibou) to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own YouTube channel.

  • It does not collect, store, or share with third parties any data of anyone other than the owner.
  • Google user data is used only within the scope of the YouTube Data API (uploading videos, thumbnails and captions, and checking them).
  • The Google authorization token (OAuth token) is stored only on the owner's local computer and is never sent to any external server.
  • The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account settings.
